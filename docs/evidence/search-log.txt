Selection and verification
- Core methods generate/edit visual assets, scenes or dynamic content, or integrate these capabilities into XR. Layout generation and retrieval-based assembly remain distinct from novel geometry generation.
- Representative selection sought contrasts in output, mechanism, representation and operating stage. PartCrafter replaced ProlificDreamer to include part structure; this was not a quality rejection. No complete candidate universe or saturation claim is reconstructed.
- Neighboring surveys and evaluation benchmarks are separate record types. The iSQoE study assesses static stereoscopic images, not runtime world generation or sustained movement comfort.
- Historical initial retrieval excluded MDPI sources. This restriction was removed in the final targeted recovery below. General application news, unverified secondary accounts, generic conversational agents, and reconstruction/compression-only work were not included as core generative methods.
- Titles, DOI/arXiv identifiers and versions resolve duplicate records. WorldPlay/HY-World 1.5 and Voyager/HunyuanWorld-Voyager each occur once.
- Publication identity and the inspected technical version remain separate. Public preprints, technical reports, official system releases and peer-reviewed papers retain their source labels. Access failures do not establish nonpublication.
- The bounded manuscript review rechecked S01 hybrid capability sources and proxy-guided MR (Sections 5.4 and 5.6) and S06 model/downstream and intervention-based evaluation (Sections 4.2.5-4.2.6). These are acknowledged overlaps, not absent topics claimed as novel.
- Official export implementations were read at versioned commits. Implementation evidence does not imply execution, export-quality validation or headset deployment. Code locators and commit dates are retained in the structured provenance.
- E indexes a named empirically examined property. Text/image adherence, pose accuracy and explicitly measured agency are different constructs; general satisfaction or affective dominance alone is not generation-control evidence.
- Runtime E requires a located timing comparison, cost profile, configuration experiment, or repeated/averaged measurement. Isolated approximate stage reports remain C with their values and conditions preserved; this does not assert that the authors did not measure them. Generation throughput, decoder time, fixed-content rendering and input-to-display latency remain distinct.
- NR means evidence was not located in the inspected material. It is not a field-wide absence claim. Codes are not combined into a readiness score, and evidence from one configuration is not inherited by another.
