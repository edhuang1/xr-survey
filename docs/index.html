01 / OVERVIEW

What can generated content
actually do in XR?

A generated object can look convincing in one view. Placing it, walking around it, editing its parts, and returning to an accepted change require different structures and controls.

This survey traces visual generation into the operations an extended reality application needs. It connects generated outputs, exposed representations, supporting components, and tested configurations across assets, spatial scenes, temporal content, world models, and XR systems.
